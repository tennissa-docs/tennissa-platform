# EFTC "SGO software" checklist vs your plan

Source: https://eftccredit.com/learn/sgo-software (read 2026-11-12). The page names one vendor, SGO HQ (the product being cloned), and promotes it; the publisher links SGO HQ in its footer, so treat it as a vendor page, not an independent review. Its 11-point checklist is still a useful bar. Its regulatory statements are its own and need checking against Treasury's guidance.

Gap numbers used in conversation: 1 = IRS report + statements, 2 = residency, 3 = substantial contributors, 4 = duplicate expense, 5 = fourth income method, 6 = partial designation, 7 = payout progress + annual certification.
