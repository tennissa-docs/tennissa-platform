## Where SGOone is ahead (ideas to consider)
- Education and onboarding for people starting an SGO (learning center, state readiness checklists).
- SMS and communications tooling.
- Smartphone document capture ("Smart Scan").
- Task assignment, priorities and deadlines for staff.
- Rule-versioned decisions: each eligibility decision records which rule version produced it.

## Where your plan is ahead
- Real money movement, ledger, 90% test, receipts, refunds, audit package, permanent records, auditor and committee roles, school verification.
