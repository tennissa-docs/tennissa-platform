# Privacy commitments

*Draft summary for review by counsel. The full privacy notice must be written with counsel before launch.*

1. **We never sell personal information.** Not to anyone, for any price.
2. **We never use it for advertising**, and never to market to students or families.
3. **We collect only what the tax-credit program requires**: what we need to receipt gifts, check eligibility, pay schools, and keep audit-ready books.
4. **Each person sees only their own information.** Donors, families and schools each see their own records; staff see what their role requires, and every view of sensitive records is logged.
5. **Sensitive numbers and documents are encrypted** beyond the standard protection, and screens show only the last four digits of tax and bank numbers.
6. **We tell you within 24 hours** of confirming that anyone gained unauthorized access to your information.
7. **We keep records only as long as our policy says** (see data retention), and explain what is kept when you ask us to delete something.
8. **Our providers** are listed in the subprocessor list, and we tell customers before adding a new one that would handle personal information.
9. **Questions:** privacy@[tennissa domain].
