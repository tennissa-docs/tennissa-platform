# Data retention policy

*Draft for review by counsel and TENNISSA's CPA.*

| record | kept | how deletion is prevented |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Donations, receipts, refund receipts, refunds, year-end statements, IRS donor reports | **permanently** | database refuses deletion and edits; files in locked storage |
| Awards, payment batches, payments, per-student lines, ACH files, remittance advice, vendor bills, invoices, proof of delivery, W-9s, bank-detail history, bank statement lines | **permanently** | same |
| The ledger (journal entries, postings), annual certifications, audit log, sign-in log, restore drills | **permanently** | append-only in the database |
| Applications, income checks, school verifications, committee recommendations, recusals | **permanently** (they back the award) | database refuses deletion |
| Family and applicant documents (tax returns, W-2s, pay stubs, benefit letters, foster placement, other-income proof) | **5 years** from the decision (or round close if never decided), then deleted automatically and the deletion logged | database refuses earlier deletion; storage locked for 5 years |
| Marketing contacts, roster contacts not tied to an application | until no longer needed or on request | — |

**Requests to delete personal information** cannot override these periods, because the records are needed for tax-credit compliance and audit. We explain what is kept, why and until when, and delete anything not covered above.
