# Staff access policy

*Draft for review.*

- **Least access.** Each person gets the narrowest role that fits their work: owner, administrator, scholarship committee member, auditor, school administrator. Roles are reviewed every quarter by an owner; unused access is removed.
- **Two-step sign-in** is required for owners and administrators.
- **Bank details and payment batches** follow the two-person rules in the platform: whoever enters bank details cannot approve them; optional second approval for every batch.
- **Leaving.** Access is revoked the day someone leaves or changes role; their past actions stay in the audit log.
- **Auditors** get read-only access granted by an owner, with an end date no more than one year away; family documents only if an owner switches them on.
- **Production access** (database, hosting, storage, keys) is limited to the technical lead and one owner as backup, with two-step sign-in on every provider account. Direct database access is used only for incidents and restore drills, and each use is noted in the incident or drill record.
- **Devices.** Staff use devices with screen lock, disk encryption and current updates; no family documents downloaded to personal devices.
- **Training.** Everyone with access reads the security overview and this policy before getting access, and yearly after.
